About Me

Hi welcome to my corner of the internet

My name is Shaniah Johnson, and I'm a beauty, wellness, and lifestyle content creator. I specialize in producing 4K, high-quality UGC and product review videos that feel authentic and relatable. My content currently reaches 50,000+ monthly views across Instagram and other platforms, and I've worked with over 20 beauty and lifestyle brands like Estée Lauder, The Ordinary, Cetaphil, Prose, and Huda Beauty, Pyrex, Febreze and so many more! I love to create UGC (user generated content) whether it's an engaging Instagram reel or simply an aesthetic product review. Whatever it is, I'd be more than happy to tailor a customized post to achieve your campaign goals. Please let me know if you'd like me to send over a content mood board, my media kit or just some creative concepts of what I can offer you!
